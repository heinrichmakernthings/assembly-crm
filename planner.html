Know what the numbers mean.

Assembly revenue = subscriptions + ticketing fees + membership fees + messaging charges. Client ticket turnover is not Assembly revenue.

Retained contribution = revenue − assumed direct delivery costs − salesperson commission. The overheads view also deducts your annual fixed-cost allowance. Neither is after-tax profit.

Proposed default commissions: 30% of subscriptions, 15% of Assembly’s ticket fee and 25% of membership/messaging gross profit. Default gross margins: 80% on subscription/ticketing and 50% on memberships/messaging. Edit these to match actual terms and delivery costs.

No churn, refunds, bad debt, tax, setup fees or additional acquisition costs are modelled. Include central salaries and unmodelled onboarding/support in overheads without counting direct costs twice. First-year timing is an estimate, not a cash-flow schedule. Sales pace is a workload target, not evidence that the market will convert.
